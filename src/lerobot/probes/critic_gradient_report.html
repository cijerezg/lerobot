Elapsed fraction and seconds remaining

elapsed fraction = (frame − segment start) / (segment end − segment start)
seconds remaining = (1 − elapsed fraction) × segment duration

Within one segment these are equivalent timing coordinates. Across segments the scale changes: at 50%, a 4-second segment has 2 seconds left; a 40-second segment has 20 seconds left. Pooling segments can therefore give different curves and correlations.

This is elapsed recorded time, not a physical completion estimate. Critic segments fold release into the preceding move, so “move” and “release” labels occupy different portions of the same timing interval. Filtering to an action can leave sparse late or early bins.
