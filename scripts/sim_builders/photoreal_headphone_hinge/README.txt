What changed
------------
The previous three open-case scenes each contained only one half: a loaded base
in ep1/2, an empty lid in ep3. This pass supplies the missing partner, joins it
with a broad fabric hinge, and places the folded headphones in one half only.
Both halves share a matched silhouette with textile exterior, zipper edging,
stitch detail, recessed gray liner, and molded impressions in the empty lid.
The headphones have a wider padded headband, shaped earcups and swivel arms;
a cable coil sits behind a small pocket. The closed ep5 case also has separate
upper/lower shells, a zipper seam and fabric hinge.
Sources: the existing frames in photoreal_objects/references/
headphones_{000,015,030,050,070,085}.jpg from sim/headphones.mp4, plus real top
images for episodes 1–3. Fabric textures are reused from the accepted object pass.
The shell proportions and placement are approximate; no exact scan is claimed.
The table, other objects, cameras and lighting are unchanged from the prior pass.

Physics and scope
-----------------
This is a visual upgrade. No articulated simulation joint or new collider is
added. The original simplified collider for the single anchor half remains;
the newly added half has no collision response. Both rendered halves follow
the original rigid body during normal simulation. The lid is static and open
in ep1/2/3 and static and closed in ep5.
The separate hinge_motion movie animates only visual geom transforms around the
fold axis and bends the cloth bridge using a half-angle transform. It is a
kinematic illustration, not a simulated closing policy, fabric simulation, or
contact test. The comparison/Rerun contain static-body camera orbits, not a policy
rollout. This keeps the previous simulation dynamics intact while fixing the
visible case structure requested for this pass.

Preservation and checks
-----------------------
Original assets and all previous experiment files are read-only dependencies.
original_sha256.json/validation.json verify they are unchanged and check exact
mass, inertia, joint, actuator and collision equality in all four layouts.
Native RGB and depth observation shapes/dtypes are checked too.
video_validation.json records final media lengths, frame counts, full-decode
checks and Rerun readability. No checkpoint policy evaluation was run.
