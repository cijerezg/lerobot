Changes
-------
13 named object variants / 20 instances across nail_ep5, nail_ep1, nail_ep2,
and nail_ep3. All accepted clipper improvements are retained.
- Bottles have shaped shoulders, ribbed caps, and real photographed labels.
- The spray bottle has its dark body, label, neck, pump and nozzle.
- The pouch has photographic fabric, blue binding and a zipper.
- Headphone cases have fabric, liners, rims, zippers, handles, and simplified
  earcups/headband/cable in the open base.
- The organizer has an open translucent lid, compartments, plugs and screws.
- The cup is tapered with real photographed print, an open interior and rim.
- The tape roll has layered film and a hollow cardboard core.
- The small knob has a rounded rubber form.
Native MuJoCo rendering is used in both columns with matching lighting.
Geometry is reference-guided and approximate, not a reconstructed precision scan.

Sources and approximations
--------------------------
The existing real recordings supply bottle/cup print and object shape references.
assets/texture_sources.json records exact source paths, quadrilaterals and crops.
The yellow label is from episode 1 wrist frame 1001; the orange label from
10-16 episode 2 wrist frame 2650. Spray and cup crops come from existing top
frames. The fabric weave comes from sim/headphones.mp4 at 30 seconds, with broad
lighting removed and mirrored tiling. references/ contains sampled real frames.
Label backs that were not observed are plain label-colored surfaces. The generic
white lying bottle currently reuses the orange vitamin label as an approximation.
Cup/spray print is limited by the source image resolution. Small hardware, seams,
and headphone components are modeled approximations guided by those references.
No external stock assets or generated lettering were used.

Preservation and validation
---------------------------
All work is in this directory. The original simulator and previous clipper pass
are untouched. 236 input-file hashes are checked in validate.py.
Old visual geoms are retained in hidden group 5 because some contribute inferred
mass. New visible geometry is in group 2, mass zero, with collisions disabled.
All four physical models have exactly matching mass, inertia, joints, actuators,
and collision geometry/settings. RGB and depth observation contracts are checked.
The original simplified colliders remain: cup/tape holes, extended handles, and
the organizer's raised lid are visual only. This pass does not add their contacts.
replay_validation.json records joint-position agreement and RGB observation timing.
validation.json records model checks; video_validation.json records media checks.
This is a visual comparison and recorded-target replay, not a policy evaluation
or a claim of improved task success / sim-to-real transfer.

Reproduce from workspace root
-----------------------------
References and texture crops are already saved. Rebuild textures if desired:
  .venv/bin/python -B lerobot/scripts/sim_builders/photoreal_objects/textures.py
Build scenes, validate, and render:
  .venv/bin/python -B lerobot/scripts/sim_builders/photoreal_objects/build.py
  MUJOCO_GL=egl .venv/bin/python -B lerobot/scripts/sim_builders/photoreal_objects/validate.py
  MUJOCO_GL=egl .venv/bin/python -B lerobot/scripts/sim_builders/photoreal_objects/render.py
render.py --quick overwrites the same outputs with a short preview.
Original assets, previous clipper assets and real recordings are read-only
runtime/build dependencies; this is not a standalone portable package.
Scene/config paths are absolute and regenerated by build.py after relocation.
