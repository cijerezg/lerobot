Drag a point in the large view to move that one token. Shift-drag to lasso points, Shift-Alt-drag to drop them. Drag the handle to move, turn or scale what is picked; every later layer, the action expert and the tip path are computed again as you drag. In an episode the same move is applied at every time step: the faint points and lines are the episode as recorded. Through training the steps are the checkpoints: without a move the second set of points is the same frame under the checkpoint chosen at "with" (faint, or in its own colour with Colour = Checkpoint); with a move the faint points are the checkpoint on the screen as recorded. Drag elsewhere, or right-drag anywhere, to orbit, wheel to zoom. Space plays, ← → step through time, ↑ ↓ through the layers, L goes back to the moved layer, S spins. Esc drops the pick, Backspace undoes every move.
